How it works

No forms, no per-site setup. Three steps.

1

Snap photos

Add one or more photos of your item. That's the only thing you have to provide.

2

Confirm what we found

We identify the exact product and suggest a price from recent sold listings. You correct anything that's off — on one screen.

3

We list it everywhere

One tap hands it to the Crosslist Connector Chrome extension, which lists it on eBay, Grailed, Depop, Poshmark, Etsy, and Reverb from your logged-in browser sessions. You watch each one go live. Facebook Marketplace posting is handled via Muse chat.

Nothing is posted until you confirm. Store names belong to their respective owners.
